THEA BOOKS / CASE STUDY

From business workflow to digital system.

01 / PROBLEM

Business records can become difficult to manage.

Sales, purchases, expenses, inventory, customers and vendor records may be maintained separately. This can make business information harder to monitor and organize.

02 / SOLUTION

One organized business management platform.

THEA Books brings key business workflows together so that transactions and records can be managed from a structured system.

03 / MODULES

Connected business modules.

Dashboard, Sales, Purchases, Expenses, Customers, Vendors, Inventory, Accounting and Reports form the core structure of the platform.

04 / FUTURE

Designed for further development.

Future development can include stronger cloud storage, multi-company support, authentication improvements, advanced reports and additional automation.
